Chef's Special & News

Chef Arjun's Butter Chicken

This month our head chef brings back his signature butter chicken, a recipe passed down from his grandmother's kitchen in Amritsar. Chicken is marinated overnight in yoghurt and spices, charred in the tandoor and then simmered in a velvety tomato and butter gravy finished with kasuri methi.

Available only on weekends till the end of the month. Pair it with our butter naan and jeera rice for the full experience.
